VEYORA TRAVEL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026. This page is a general operational privacy notice and should be reviewed for the jurisdictions in which VEYORA operates before full commercial launch.

Information we collect

VEYORA may collect contact details, trip preferences, booking information, communications, payment-status information and technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

How information is used

Information may be used to prepare travel requests, provide customer support, communicate booking updates, process or reconcile payments, prevent misuse, and improve VEYORA services.

Travel suppliers and service providers

When necessary to arrange travel, relevant information may be shared with airlines, hotels, travel suppliers, payment providers and technology providers involved in delivering the requested service.

Payment information

VEYORA should use approved payment processors for card payments. VEYORA's application should not intentionally store complete card numbers or card security codes.

Data retention and security

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for operational, contractual, legal, accounting and security purposes. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect it.

Your choices

Depending on applicable law, customers may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or other controls over personal information. Requests can be directed through VEYORA's contact channel.

Updates

This notice may be updated as VEYORA's services, suppliers and legal obligations evolve.
