Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about VEYORA multi-city planning, pricing, payments and travel documents.

How does VEYORA multi-city planning work?

Choose your departure airport, destinations, dates and number of nights. VEYORA builds the journey as one travel request rather than separate disconnected bookings.

Are prices final?

Flight and hotel inventory can change until services are confirmed and ticketed. A displayed estimate or quote is subject to supplier availability and its stated validity period.

Does VEYORA charge hidden fees?

VEYORA aims to show the customer package total clearly before payment. Supplier-imposed taxes, resort fees or local charges that must be paid locally should be disclosed when known.

When do I receive vouchers and invoices?

After the relevant services are confirmed, available documents appear in your private booking-status page.

Can I cancel a trip?

You can submit a cancellation request from your booking-status page. Refundability and penalties depend on airline, hotel and other supplier rules.

How can I track my request?

Use your VEYORA public reference and private tracking token on the Track Booking page.
